And the Lenovo has a dedicated PINK mic jack: codec node 0x18 reads [Jack] Mic at Ext Rear, Conn = 1/8, Color = Pink, with 30 dB of Mic Boost that was sitting unused at 0 dB. Your step, and you are the expert for it: put the DMM on that pink jack, ring to sleeve, DC volts, machine awake. ~2–5 V = plug-in power is there and the kit’s XLR→3.5 mm cable goes straight in, $0. Near 0 V = use the small USB sound card that these kits usually ship in the box. Then run the Lenovo test script and it measures the capture for real. Do not build a phantom supply and do not buy an interface — neither is needed. Nothing is owed until both free routes fail (then $3.49–$12.95, priced 11-10).
