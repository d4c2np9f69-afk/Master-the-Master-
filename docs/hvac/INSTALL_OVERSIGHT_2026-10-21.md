## 7. 🌡️ WHAT THE GARAGE VENT WILL ACTUALLY DO — set expectations now

From the 09-17 analysis, calibrated on **Jeff's own measurement** (94 °F with no AC; ~80 °F with
the LG portable):

| Setup | Garage settles at |
|---|---|
| Nothing (today) | **94 °F** |
| LG portable alone | ~80 °F |
| **7" vent alone, damper open** | **~84 °F** |
| **7" vent + the LG** | **~73–74 °F** |

The 7" branch carries **~125 CFM**, about **13% of the unit's capacity**. On a brutal afternoon,
**close the damper at the register** and the full airflow goes back to the house. Winter: same
damper, closed. The garage has no return, so it pressurises and leaks out around the door —
the right direction.

**Do not expect a cold garage from the vent alone.** It takes ~10 °F off. If Jeff later wants a
room he can work in all afternoon, **R-38 blown cellulose over the garage ceiling (~10 bags,
$120–150, an afternoon)** roughly doubles what the vent does — the ceiling is ~⅔ of the heat gain.
