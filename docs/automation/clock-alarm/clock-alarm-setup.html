Grandfather Clock Alarm Setup

Home Assistant plays the chimes, voice and alarms straight out of its own audio jack into the QFX speaker behind the clock, over one cable run under the floor. A Tuya plug switches the two 12 V sirens. No Beast, no Bluetooth, no extra board.

1 · Signal & power chain

Audio (3.5 mm) 120 V mains +12 V (red wire) − / ground (black wire) Wi-Fi control

2 · Where it all goes

Simplified from the house plan; distances from it: clock to guest bed ~22 ft, kitchen ~23 ft, master bed ~28–29 ft. The front-door aim sends the sound down the foyer, where both bedroom doors open.

Install order

  1. JeffRun the 50 ft cable: HA box jack → down through the floor → crawl space → up behind the clock → QFX AUX IN.
  2. JeffSnip the 3.5 mm plug off each siren lead and strip ¼ in (so nothing 12 V can ever be plugged into the speaker's AUX jack). Wire both to the screw terminal (reds +, blacks −), plug in the 12 V adapter, plug the adapter into the Tuya plug.
  3. JeffAdd the Tuya plug in the Smart Life app so it shows up in HA.
  4. ClaudeSwitch HA's audio from HDMI to Analog, set up VLC playback, and move the chimes, voice and alarms from the Beast to HA.
  5. ClaudeMake the Tuya plug the siren switch; tornado and break-in turn it on, everything else stays voice-only.
  6. BothLoudness test when Angela isn't resting: knob at max, Apple Watch Noise app at your pillow, door shut. Target is 75 dB or more.

The Beast keeps playing the clock over Bluetooth until the new path is proven.
