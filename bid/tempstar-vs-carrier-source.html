The short answer: it is the same machine with a different sticker. Tempstar is ICP (International Comfort Products), a Carrier Global company. The two product-data books are the same 39-page document - same page order, same tables, same numbers on every rating row, same accessory part numbers - with "Carrier" swapped for "Tempstar" and "Puron Advance" swapped for "R-454B". Of 750 text lines in the Tempstar book, 596 appear word-for-word in the Carrier book; the rest are brand names and page headers. What actually differs is the WARRANTY and what each BIDDER wraps around it.

Bottom line.
  • Hardware: no reason to prefer one box over the other - same coils, same blower, same compressor, same stainless heat exchanger, same sound, same weight, same charge. The only physical difference is a wire coil guard vs louvered panels around the outdoor coil.
  • Factory warranty: the Tempstar is slightly better - it carries a 3-year one-time whole-unit replacement the 48NL does not have. Parts and heat exchanger terms are identical (register within 90 days or you lose half of it).
  • Money over 10 years: within about $20 of each other once Goodlettsville's permit and 10-yr labour are added - the $1,510 sticker gap is really the labour warranty and the permit, not the unit.
  • So the decision is the contractor, not the brand: who pulls the permit, who records the static pressures, who fabricates the plenums, who installs the garage run as drawn, who has the licence, who backs the labour. That is exactly what the bid gates measure.
